DESCRIPCIÓN
I get asked for my recipe almost every day, so here it is, for free. Save this.

Mix 1 cup flour with ½ cup fine salt.
Add about ½ cup warm water, a little at a time, until it feels like soft play dough.
Add 10–15 drops of essential oil. Lavender is the easiest one to start with.
Press it into a silicone mold.
Push a few dried flowers gently into the top.
Let it dry 24–48 hours, then pop it out.

That's it. You probably have everything in your kitchen right now.

Now look at the photo. On the left is my very first batch, made with exactly this recipe. Beige, a little lumpy, one cracked while it dried, and the flower turned brown in four days. I loved them. But nobody was going to pay $12 for those.

On the right is what I make now. Same kitchen table. Same mold.

The difference wasn't the recipe. It was the things nobody tells you: why they crack while drying and how to stop it, how to get them bright white instead of beige, which flowers keep their color, how to make the scent last for weeks, and how to wrap them so they look like a boutique gift.

I learned all of that from a $27 guide. I made it back on my very first sale, a gift set for $40.

Now most of mine go as gift sets: teacher gifts, baby showers, bridesmaids, coworkers at Christmas. And the holidays are right around the corner.

DESCRIPCIÓN
Every year around this time, people start asking me how I make these. So here's the basic recipe, for free. Save this for your holiday gifts.

Mix 1 cup flour with ½ cup fine salt.
Add about ½ cup warm water, a little at a time, until it feels like soft play dough.
Add 10–15 drops of essential oil. Lavender is the easiest to start with.
Press it into a silicone mold.
Push a few dried flowers gently into the top.
Let it dry 24–48 hours, then pop it out.

That's it. One evening at your kitchen table and you've got gifts for half your list.

Now, the honest part. If you follow only this recipe, you'll get something sweet and clearly homemade. Perfect for your mom. Not something someone will pay $12 for.
