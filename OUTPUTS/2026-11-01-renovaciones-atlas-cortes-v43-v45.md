Guion 43

EN
The same hour in your chair is worth double between now and New Year's Eve, and you are about to sell it at trim prices.
Every woman who books between now and then wants a change, not a tidy up. And she asks for it by name. Hush cut. Butterfly. Bixie. Long bob.
If those are not cuts you take, you spend the best six weeks of your year doing maintenance, while the chair down the road books out and keeps those women in January.
It is not your technique. It is that the sequence is never in your head at the moment somebody is already sitting down.
Twenty-five Cut Maps. One page per cut, sectioning, elevation and guide section, checked in seconds mid-service.
Open it tonight. Put two of those cuts on your menu tomorrow.

Guion 45

EN
There is a woman in your book this week that you are going to work for free.
She is the one coming back because one side sat differently, or the layers shelved, or the fringe went heavy on the left. You fit her in, you do not charge her, and that hour is gone.
That is the money nobody counts. It never shows up as a loss, it shows up as a full week that paid you like a quiet one.
And the cut was not the problem. The direction and the weight were decided before you ever picked up your shears.
Twenty-five Cut Maps. You read the page, you hold the elevation it gives you, you hand her the mirror once. Less reworking, time back.
Open it tonight and give that slot back to yourself this week.
