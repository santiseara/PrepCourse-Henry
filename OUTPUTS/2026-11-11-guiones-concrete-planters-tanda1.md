## Guion 5 - Tu primera maceta se va a rajar y no es culpa tuya

EN

Your first concrete planter is going to crack, and it will have nothing to do with talent.
Three things break them, and they are always the same three.
Too much water, because the mix pours easier and everyone does it. A demold two hours too early, because you want to see it. And no cure, because nobody tells you the piece keeps hardening for days after it looks finished.
Fix those and the same bag of cement starts giving you pieces that are light, smooth on the inside, and strong enough to sit outside all winter.
It is a ratio, a clock and a method. Not a skill you are born with.
The guide has the ratio card, the cure times for each size, and the mistake map with the photo of what each one looks like so you can tell what went wrong.
Tap Learn more and get the first one right instead of the fourth.

ES

Tu primera maceta de cemento se va a rajar, y no tiene nada que ver con el talento.
Hay tres cosas que las rompen, y son siempre las mismas tres.
Demasiada agua, porque la mezcla se vuelca más fácil y lo hace todo el mundo. Desmoldar dos horas antes, porque querés verla. Y no curarla, porque nadie te cuenta que la pieza sigue endureciendo varios días después de que parece terminada.
